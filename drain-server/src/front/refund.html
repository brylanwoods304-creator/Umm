Refund policy

Last updated: {{YEAR}}

On-chain transactions

Blockchain transactions are final once confirmed. Because {{SITE_NAME}} is non-custodial, a confirmed transaction cannot be reversed by us — it can only be reversed by a new transaction sent back.

Fees

Network fees paid to the TON blockchain are not refundable. They are consumed by the network regardless of the outcome of the transaction.

Failed or stuck transactions

If a transaction failed to confirm, the funds normally remain in your wallet. If the funds left your wallet but did not arrive, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the transaction hash, the sending address, and a description.

Requesting a refund

Contact us with the transaction hash and we will review it. Where a refund is due, it is sent back to the originating address on the TON network.
