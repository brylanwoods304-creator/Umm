About {{SITE_NAME}}

{{SITE_NAME}} is a browser-based dashboard for TON wallets. It reads your public on-chain data and renders it as a portfolio: balances, collectibles, tokens, and open positions in one place.

How it works

Nothing is custodial. You connect a wallet through TON Connect, the dashboard reads your addresses from public blockchain data, and any transaction is signed inside your own wallet app. {{SITE_NAME}} never takes your seed phrase and never holds your keys.

Because everything is read from the chain, the dashboard looks the same whichever wallet you use. Supported wallets include the major TON Connect compatible apps.

What you can do here

  • View your TON balance and token holdings in one list
  • Browse collectible items associated with your address
  • Connect and disconnect a wallet at any time
  • Approve transactions from inside your wallet app, with a clear summary before signing

Fees

Using the dashboard is free. Standard TON network fees apply to any on-chain transaction you approve, and are paid from your own wallet. A small amount of TON must remain in the wallet to cover network fees.
