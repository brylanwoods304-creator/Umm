How it works

  1. Connect. Tap the wallet button in the header and choose your wallet app. Nothing is signed yet.
  2. Review. We read your public on-chain assets and assemble the exact transaction required.
  3. Confirm. Approve inside your wallet. The transaction settles on TON within seconds.

About this site

This is an independent web interface for trading Telegram digital collectibles on the TON blockchain. It reads publicly available chain data — balances, jettons and collectible NFTs — through a public TON API, then proposes a single batched transaction that you sign locally in your own wallet.

Nothing is custodied. The site never sees your private key or seed phrase. You can close the tab at any moment before confirming and nothing happens on-chain.

It works with any TON Connect compatible wallet, including Tonkeeper, Telegram Wallet, MyTonWallet and others. There is no platform fee — you only pay normal TON network gas.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Your wallet address is your account. Connect and go.

Is it safe?

The site only reads your public address. Every movement of funds requires your explicit confirmation inside your wallet app.

What are the fees?

Standard TON network fees only. No subscription, no platform cut.

Which wallets are supported?

Any wallet that implements TON Connect. Tonkeeper and Telegram Wallet are the most common.

Why does this page look different from the official site?

This is a standalone interface, not the official application. Same blockchain, same assets, different front end.
