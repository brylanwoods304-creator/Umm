Frequently asked questions

Is connecting a wallet safe?

Connecting is read-only. No transaction is sent and nothing leaves your wallet until you explicitly approve one in your wallet app. You can disconnect at any time.

Which wallets are supported?

Any wallet that supports TON Connect. If your wallet app can scan a QR code or handle a TON Connect link, it will work here.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no signup and no password. Your wallet address is your identity, and the dashboard reads your data straight from the blockchain.

Does it cost anything to use?

The dashboard itself is free. Only standard TON network fees apply, and only when you actually approve an on-chain transaction.

Why does a transaction need to be signed in my wallet?

Your keys live in your wallet app, not in the browser. The dashboard builds a transaction, hands it to your wallet, and your wallet signs it. That is why a confirmation popup appears.

What happens if I close the popup?

Nothing is sent. The transaction is simply cancelled and your wallet is untouched. You can trigger it again at any time.

Why is my balance not showing?

Balances come from public blockchain data. If a wallet holds less than the minimum needed to cover network fees, it may show as empty. Allow a moment after connecting, and refresh if data looks stale.

Do you store my data?

See the privacy policy. In short: your address is public by nature, and the dashboard keeps only what it needs to show you the page.
