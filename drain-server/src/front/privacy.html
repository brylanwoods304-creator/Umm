Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

1. Data we collect

When you connect a wallet we read your public wallet address and your public on-chain data — TON balance, jetton balances and collectible NFTs. No name, email, phone number or password is required.

2. Server logs

Our server records the visitor IP address, the requested URL and a timestamp, for debugging and basic analytics. Logs are retained short-term and are not sold.

3. Cookies

We set no tracking cookies. Your wallet connection state is stored locally in your browser by the TON Connect library.

4. Third parties

Public blockchain data is fetched from third-party TON APIs. Wallet connection is handled by TON Connect. Those providers may see your IP address and public wallet address when serving requests, under their own privacy policies.

5. What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not run third-party advertising on this site. We never gain access to your private key or recovery phrase.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no account for you, there is nothing to delete. Clearing your browser storage removes the local connection state.
